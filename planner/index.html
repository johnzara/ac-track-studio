    Routing rules

    The directions service (Valhalla, as on openstreetmap.org) answers in about a second but does not drive on race circuits (highway=raceway) or footpaths: use the downloaded roads for those. If the service is down, the planner downloads the roads by itself. A stop's leg can also be a direct line for a stretch that is not in OSM.
